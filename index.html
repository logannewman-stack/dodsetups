FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does buying work?

Hit Buy, send the order form, and we confirm your build within one business day. You pay by invoice (card or ACH) — half down to start the build, the balance at pickup or delivery. Most rigs are ready in 5–7 days.

Is the rig installed into my vehicle?

Every rig comes assembled, plumbed and tested as a unit, with the mounting hardware for your vehicle class — bed straps and a weather cover for trucks, floor anchors for vans, a trunk layout that doesn’t slide for sedans. Local pickup includes a load-in session where we mount it with you and run the whole system before you leave.

Why these parts? They look cheap.

That’s the point — this is the least money that produces a rig that fully works, chosen from gear we’ve actually run: Predator generators, Sun Joe pressure washers, pancake compressors, fleet-supply poly tanks. Every part carries its manufacturer warranty, and every one has a standard upgrade path — bigger tank, gas pressure washer, quieter generator — when your route fills up.

What do I still need on my own?

The vehicle, gas for the generator, and water. Fill the tank at home before your route — every rig includes the fill fittings. That’s it: soap, towels, brushes, buckets and dressings are all in the loadout.

Can I split the payment?

Yes. Active DOD contractors can split any rig over their first jobs — typically 4 weekly payments with the rig released at the first payment. Note it on the order form and we’ll set it up on the invoice.

What if something breaks?

Text us first — most fixes are a fitting or a fuse and we’ll talk you through it same-day. Defective parts go back under their manufacturer warranty (90 days to 2 years depending on the part), and we keep the common spares — pump seals, hose ends, couplers — on the shelf so you’re not down for a week.

I don’t see my vehicle in the finder.

The finder covers the common US fleet back to 1990, but if yours isn’t listed, order the rig that matches your cargo space — trunk = Sedan rig, hatch = SUV rig, open bed = Truck rig, sliding door = Minivan rig, enclosed cargo = Small or Large Van rig — or put your year/make/model in the order form notes and we’ll spec it for you.
